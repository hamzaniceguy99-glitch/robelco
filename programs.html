Program 01 · Inspected, then used

We book it, correct anything recorded, arrange re-inspection and hand over the report. A contractor who finishes and leaves the inspection as somebody else's problem has left you with an installation you cannot lawfully use.

Program 02 · Both credentials held

Cooling and air conditioning

This is where the two-part requirement bites. The technician needs a certificate of the right category and the company needs to be registered as a refrigeration undertaking. Many installers hold one and assume it covers the other. We hold both and will show you both, and we carry out the leak checking that applies above the thresholds.

Electric heating and ventilation

On premiums we state the specification we will install and nothing more: entitlement is the granting authority's decision and conditions change, so promising a grant is not ours to do.

Program 04 · Load-bearing is different

Metal structure repair

Repairing a gate and repairing something that holds a load are different jobs with different rules. Where a component is load-bearing and falls within the harmonised standard for structural steelwork, the requirements on factory production control, execution class and welding coordination apply. We say which category an item is in rather than implying certification across everything.
